01 / RESEARCH

Images are data.
Data opens possibilities.

Recognition, learning and interaction methods, applied across six complementary areas.

01

Multimodal biometrics

Recognition from images and physiological signals: face, iris, hand, gait, EEG and ECG. Data quality, fusion and decision reliability.

Recognition · Fusion · In-the-wild
02

AI for medical imaging

Ultrasound image segmentation and classification. Multi-scale ensembles and generative models for MRI volume resolution enhancement.

Ultrasound · Segmentation · Generative AI
03

Intelligent mobility

Curriculum and reinforcement learning for autonomous driving. Traffic analysis, 3D detection and video surveillance for smart cities.

Autonomous driving · CARLA · Smart cities
04

Sports analytics & interaction

Football performance analysis, wearable sensors and multimodal annotation. Conversational systems connecting domain experts and AI.

Human–AI · Multimodal interfaces · Football
05

From time series to images

Visual encodings and convolutional networks for time-series analysis and financial market forecasting.

Gramian Angular Fields · CNN ensembles
06

Digital cultural heritage

Fresco reassembly and digital anastylosis. Computer vision, virtual and augmented reality for cultural heritage reconstruction and access.

SAFFO · DAFNE · Image matching

All research interests and methodological contributions ↗

Methods and people at the centre.

Multimodal fusion, deep learning, generative models and reinforcement learning. Collaboration across universities, laboratories and industry to turn research questions into systems that can be evaluated.
