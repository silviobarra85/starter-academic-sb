Research interests8
Biometrics and multimodal recognition. Face, iris, periocular, ear, hand and gait recognition; physiological EEG and ECG signals. Sample quality, feature selection, denoising, fusion and decision reliability, with a focus on mobile acquisition and unconstrained conditions.
Computer vision and machine learning. Image representation and classification, head pose estimation, facial expression recognition, incremental and 3D object detection, tracking and source camera identification. Neural models, ensembles, fuzzy methods and representative exemplar selection.
Autonomous driving, video surveillance and smart cities. Curriculum learning and deep reinforcement learning for driving; integration of segmentation and depth information. Camera-based traffic analysis, anomaly detection, licence plate recognition, speed estimation and monocular 3D detection in roadside scenarios.
Medical imaging and AI for health. Ultrasound image segmentation and classification, including breast and endometriotic lesions; multi-scale ensembles for lesion localisation. Generative models for intermediate slice synthesis and resolution enhancement of magnetic resonance imaging volumes.
Time series and financial forecasting. Time series-to-image encoding, Gramian Angular Field representations and analysis at multiple temporal resolutions; convolutional neural networks and ensembles for market forecasting and experimental evaluation of trading strategies.
Sports analytics and human–AI interaction. Football match annotation and analysis, voice and touch interfaces, wearable inertial sensors and automated annotation consistency checks. Conversational methodologies for sports performance analysis; multimodal interaction and investigation of visual question answering applications.
Cultural heritage, virtual and augmented reality. Digital reassembly of frescoes and fragments, assisted and automated anastylosis, image matching and evaluation on dedicated datasets. Virtual/augmented reality and reconstruction of objects and scenes for cultural heritage interpretation and access.
Distributed intelligent systems, edge/cloud and IoT. Cloud biometric services, continuous authentication, personalised access and edge processing of biometric data; architectures and data management for IoT and smart cities. Contrastive alignment and communication between neural experts with disjoint observations, investigated in the preprint The Dialectical Space (2026).
